Kuopio Boreal Forest Carbon Metrics & Regional Inventory
Static Metric

Carbon Storage (Stock)

The total static quantity of carbon permanently locked inside living tree wood, roots, and dense boreal soil peat layers. Represents long-term climate resilience.

Dynamic Metric

Carbon Sequestration (Sink)

The active net quantity of CO₂ absorbed from the atmosphere each year through photosynthetic growth minus harvesting and natural respiration.

1. Annual Carbon Sequestration Rate (Sink)

Average regional growth minus logging yields a net sequestration rate of roughly 3.0 to 4.5 tonnes of CO₂ per hectare per year across mixed pine/spruce stands.

2. Total Stored Carbon (Stock)

Dense Finnish boreal forests hold roughly 250 to 350 tonnes of pure Carbon per hectare (~900 to 1,300 tonnes of CO₂ eq) combined across aboveground tree biomass (~30%) and soil organic matter (~70%).

Logging Disruptions & Felling Audits

Harvesting abruptly releases significant soil & biomass CO₂. Real-time tracking of official felling permits vs actual satellite clear-cuts is critical for calculating carbon credit reversals.

Risks to Carbon Permanence

Extreme windthrow storms, Ips typographus (bark beetle) infestations, and peatland drainage alter a healthy forest stand from a net carbon sink into a net greenhouse gas emission source.
